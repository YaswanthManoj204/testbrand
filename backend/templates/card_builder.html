How to setup Facebook Chat

Connecting Facebook Messenger to your BrandNest digital business card is quick and easy:

  1. Go to your Facebook Business Page.
  2. Copy your Page ID or your Page's username / slug from the URL (e.g. facebook.com/yourbrand).
  3. Paste it into the Facebook Page ID input field in your Card Settings.
  4. Toggle Enable and click Save.

Your card will automatically display a high-converting Messenger chat bubble that opens conversations with your audience!
